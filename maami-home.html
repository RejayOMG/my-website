Product Editorial · Maami Home, 2024

Brand Strategy & Visual Identity

A visual system rooted in texture, warmth, and material honesty.

The brief called for something that felt premium without being cold — a brand that could live comfortably between editorial photography and a seamless shopping experience. I developed a visual system grounded in warm neutrals, honest materials, and generous white space.

Typography choices leaned into the serif tradition — refined but never stiff. The color palette drew from natural materials: linen, walnut, stone. Every layout decision was made to let the product breathe and speak for itself.

"Where every surface tells a story of craft and intention."

Web Design & E-Commerce

A shopping experience that feels as considered as the products themselves.

The e-commerce build was designed as an editorial journey, not a catalogue. Each product page is treated as a moment — with full-width photography, material storytelling, and a purchase flow that never rushes the customer.

Navigation is minimal and intentional. Filters are smart but hidden until needed. The checkout is frictionless. The entire experience reinforces the brand promise: that choosing Maami is choosing quality over quantity.
