OOH Campaign · São Paulo, 2024

Brand Strategy & Visual Identity

A focused system built around trust and clarity.

The reason for One focused on creating a more trustworthy, modern presence through a clear and intentional visual system. I designed a neutral color palette anchored in white and gray, using vibrant accents selectively to draw attention without distraction. The goal was to bring calm and structure to an otherwise stressful product space.

Alongside the visual work, I developed a simple but consistent tone of voice that felt human, not corporate. From layout spacing to typography choices, every decision aimed to reduce friction and make the borrowing experience feel supportive — especially for users unfamiliar with financial products.

"Apply, approve, invest and grow with confidence."

Web Design & UI

A focused, single-page layout built around conversion.

The website was built as a focused, single-page layout that guides users through the core value of the product without distraction. Each section supports a specific action — building trust, answering key questions, and prompting action with clear CTAs.

The layout was designed as a functional concept to explore how a more intentional structure could communicate the brand's values. Through a mobile-first, friendly approach, the experience makes it easy for users to go from interest to application with clarity and confidence.

Brand Guidelines

A lightweight system that scales across every touchpoint.

To support the rebrand, I created a lightweight brand manual that defines the essential visual elements — logo, colors, typography, and imagery. While not exhaustive, it provides clear direction to maintain consistency across key touchpoints and communicate the brand's identity effectively.

The system is built around a bold, geometric logotype, a balanced color palette, and clean Swiss-inspired typography. It also includes guidance on visual direction, supported by mockups showing how the identity extends across digital and physical contexts.
