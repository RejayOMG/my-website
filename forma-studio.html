Art Direction & Visual System

A visual language as precise and considered as the architecture itself.

The challenge was to create a photography and art direction system that could hold its own alongside the work it was documenting. Architecture photography is a discipline in itself — it requires an understanding of light, proportion, and restraint that mirrors the design process.

I developed a shooting brief, post-processing language, and compositional grammar that became the foundation for all of Forma's visual output — from portfolio presentations to editorial features and award submissions.

"Editorial, precise, and unapologetically minimal at every scale."

Portfolio & Digital Presence

A portfolio that frames the work — not decorates it.

The digital presence was built around a single principle: get out of the way of the work. A minimal grid system, generous whitespace, and a carefully considered type hierarchy let the architecture speak.

Project pages use full-bleed photography with restrained annotation. The navigation is near-invisible. Every interaction is deliberate. The result is a portfolio that feels less like a website and more like a curated exhibition.

Brand Guidelines

A visual system that brings order to complexity.

The brand guide codifies Forma's visual principles — grid-based layouts, monochromatic photography, and precise typographic rules. Cool slate tones and geometric marks form the foundation.

The system is built around structure and restraint — a sans-serif wordmark, a muted cool palette, and strict compositional rules for all photography and presentation output.
